### Layout Critique (6-Module Feature Section)

* **Massive Dead Space in Cards 01–03:** The top three cards (*Kasir offline*, *QRIS statis*, *Scan tanpa barcode*) contain completely empty, white rectangular preview containers taking up roughly 60% of each card's height. This creates huge dead zones and gives the top half of the section an unfinished wireframe appearance.
* **Severe Visual Asymmetry (Top Row vs. Bottom Row):** While the top three cards are empty voids, the bottom three cards are packed with rich, detailed UI mockups (interactive split image, price pills, radial health gauge). This creates an unbalanced visual weight where the bottom feels dense and interactive, while the top feels neglected.
* **Absence of Flagship Hierarchy:** All six modules are forced into identical 50/50 card dimensions. The core offline cash register (*Kasir offline*)—which is the app’s primary value proposition highlighted in the hero—receives the exact same footprint and visual prominence as secondary AI utilities like *AI foto produk*.
* **Bottom-Heavy Typography & Broken Reading Path:** In every card, the module title and explanation copy are pushed to the very bottom below the visual placeholder. In the top three cards, users must traverse a large blank void before reaching the headline, breaking standard visual hierarchy (Title $\rightarrow$ Visual $\rightarrow$ Context).
* **Uneven Internal Content Density:** The cards struggle with inconsistent internal rhythm. *QRIS statis* contains only two lines of short text under an empty box, whereas *AI penjaga harga* stacks tag pills, an active selection pill, multiple monetary values, and margin copy, making the cards feel visually discordant side-by-side.
* **Awkward Grid Margin & Header Dead Space:** The section header (*"Semua yang usahamu butuh..."*) is left-aligned to a narrow container width, leaving a massive empty void to the right before abruptly transitioning into the full-width 2-column card layout, weakening the transition into the feature set.
